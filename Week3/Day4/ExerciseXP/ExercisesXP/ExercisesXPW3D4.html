Exercise 1 — Change the article

Click the headings, hover over the title and second paragraph, and use the bold button.

Strategy: Select only elements inside this article. Remove the final paragraph, attach event listeners, and use CSS transitions for the fade-out bonus.

History of the chocolate

Chocolate is made from tropical Theobroma cacao tree seeds. Its earliest use dates back to the Olmec civilization in Mesoamerica.

After the European discovery of the Americas, chocolate became very popular in the wider world, and its demand exploded.

Chocolate has since become a popular food product that millions enjoy every day, thanks to its unique, rich, and sweet taste.

    Exercise 3 — Transform the sentence

    Move the mouse into the sentence to make all bold words blue; move out to restore black.

    Strategy: Gather all <strong> elements once and reuse that collection in two functions. Check relatedTarget on mouseout, so moving over nested bold words does not accidentally reset the colors.

    Exercise 4 — Volume of a sphere

    Input radius value and get the volume of a sphere.

    Strategy: Validate and convert the radius to a number, calculate (4 / 3) * Math.PI * radius ** 3, then show the answer in the read-only volume field. Radius 0 is valid; negative radii are not.
