EXERCISE 01

Numbers divisible by 23

Find all numbers from 0 to 500 that are divisible by your chosen divisor, then calculate their sum.

Click Calculate to see the numbers.

Try 23, 3, or 45. The original function also works with no parameter: displayNumbersDivisible().

EXERCISE 02

Shopping list

Your cart: 1 banana, 1 orange, 1 apple. Items with zero stock cannot be purchased.

Click Run myBill() to purchase the available items.

Bonus included: buying an available item reduces its stock by 1.

EXERCISE 03

What's in my wallet?

Count your coins and check whether you can afford the item.

Click Check my wallet to get true or false.
EXERCISE 04

Vacation costs

Hotel $140/night, car $40/day (5% off for more than 10 days), plus plane tickets.

Click a button to answer the 3 questions in browser popups.

Original: each cost function asks a question. Bonus: only totalVacationCostBonus() asks questions; the three other functions take parameters.

My book list
